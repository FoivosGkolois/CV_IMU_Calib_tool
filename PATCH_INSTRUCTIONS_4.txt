PATCH 4.1 — GP BAYESIAN OPTIMIZATION
- Replaces the temporary dynamic grid selector with Gaussian-process Bayesian Optimization.
- Optimizes only alpha_stable, alpha_dynamic, and max_accel_correction_deg.
- Uses deterministic replay of identical raw IMU data.
- Primary objective remains synchronized training-cycle RMSE.
- Expected Improvement selects new candidate evaluations.
- Fixed evaluation budget: 30 candidates; fixed seed for reproducibility.
- Hard candidate constraints replace weighted lag/jitter penalties.
- Static/measured quantities remain fixed and are never optimized.

PATCH 4.2 — HELD-OUT VALIDATION + UNCERTAINTY
- Final BO recommendation is evaluated once on cycles excluded from optimization.
- Reports held-out RMSE, MAE, lag and RMSE change versus the static-stage baseline.
- Reports GP posterior uncertainty.
- Adds cycle-level bootstrap 95% confidence intervals when >=2 held-out cycles exist.
- Reports marker reprojection quality as a reference-quality proxy without claiming angular uncertainty.
- Adds same-budget random-search and budgeted-grid engineering comparators for thesis evidence.
- Browser, YAML, and JSON report expose the same final recommendation and validation result.
