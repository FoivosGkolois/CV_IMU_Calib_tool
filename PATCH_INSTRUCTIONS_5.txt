BASELINE
CV_IMU_Calib_tool(20260929-145240).zip / branch V2_tags_Bayes / Patch 4 applied.

WHAT THIS PATCH DOES
1. Adds one-time known-angle validation for the full camera + two-tag reference.
2. Persists the validation result in reference_setup.yaml and reference log.
3. Freezes final routine protocol:
   - static: 12 s fully extended and still,
   - dynamic: first 12 accepted complete cycles,
   - order: 4 slow + 4 normal + 4 brisk,
   - held-out cycles: 4, 8, 12,
   - dynamic recording allowance: 60 s.
4. Freezes known-angle validation:
   - 180, 150, 120, 90 deg,
   - 5 captures per angle,
   - 1 s median capture, >=15 valid frames,
   - RMSE <=5 deg,
   - each tested angle MAE <=5 deg,
   - repeatability SD <=2 deg.
5. Adds build/source/filter/reference provenance to final run outputs.
6. Saves the exact reference_setup.yaml snapshot with every calibration run.
7. Removes stale migration wording and documents the final protocol/rationale.
8. Stops presenting container-internal 172.x addresses as browser URLs.
