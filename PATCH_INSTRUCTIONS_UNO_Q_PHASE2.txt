PHASE 2 APP
A ready App Lab package is included at:
uno_q_phase2_app_ready.zip

You can rebuild it from the current source at any time with:
13_BUILD_UNO_Q_PHASE2_APP.bat

Open/import the ready App in Arduino App Lab and start it on UNO Q.
Connect the USB camera to UNO Q. Then open from the PC browser:
http://<UNO-Q-IP>:8765

PHASE 2 DATA FLOW
UNO Q MCU: 100 Hz raw dual-IMU acquisition
Arduino Bridge: internal MCU-to-Linux transfer
UNO Q Linux: MoveNet, GUI, filtering, synchronization, ZIP recording, static/dynamic calibration
PC: browser only

IMPORTANT
This patch does not add motor control and does not replace the Phase 1 serial sketch.
Bridge throughput, camera access, MoveNet runtime installation, and actual FPS require UNO Q hardware validation.
