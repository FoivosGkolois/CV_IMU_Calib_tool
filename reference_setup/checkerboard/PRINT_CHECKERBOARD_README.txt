CAMERA CALIBRATION CHECKERBOARD

Pattern: 9 x 6 INNER corners = 10 x 7 squares
Square size: 25 mm
Board size: 250 x 175 mm
Page: A4 landscape

PRINTING
- Print checkerboard_9x6_25mm_A4_landscape.svg at 100% / Actual Size.
- Disable Fit to Page / Shrink / Scale.
- Measure one square after printing: it must be 25 x 25 mm.
- Use matte paper and keep the sheet flat on a rigid backing.
- Do not use glossy lamination.

ONE-TIME USE
1. Use the same physical camera/lens and camera profile that will be used with the AprilTags.
2. In the browser press Start checkerboard calibration.
3. Move and tilt the board through clearly different positions, distances and orientations.
4. Continue until 15/15 diverse views are accepted.
5. A successful result is saved persistently on the UNO Q and reloaded after normal power cycles.
6. Repeat only if the physical camera/lens, resolution or frame rotation changes.
